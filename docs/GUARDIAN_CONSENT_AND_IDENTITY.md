# Guardian consent and child identity — prelaunch safety design

**Status: planning scaffold only, 22 October 2026. No child enrolment, guardian verification, consent capture or child-data processing is authorised or available.** This document is an engineering and review checklist, not legal advice or a claim that a Ghanaian or international legal requirement has been satisfied. The current PostgreSQL pilot remains for **invited adults/internal reviewers only**; its adult declaration is self-attestation, not verified age. The public family and learner workspaces remain labelled browser previews with sample/local data.

## Enforced hold today

- `003_guardian_groundwork.sql` creates only `safeguarding.release_gate` and eight planning controls. It has **no child profiles, names, dates of birth, addresses, uploaded IDs, guardian evidence, consent records or minor-facing accounts**. Database `CHECK` constraints fix child accounts, child-data collection and minor learning to `false`; changing them requires a reviewed migration. The controls remain `not_ready` and cannot be marked approved by a runtime request.
- `engine:migrate` installs/verifies this hold after the adult pilot and draft-2 engine migrations. Adult draft-2 startup and operator enrolment fail closed if the hold is missing or changed. `CHILD_ACCOUNTS_ENABLED`, `GUARDIAN_EVIDENCE_COLLECTION_ENABLED` and `MINOR_LEARNING_ENABLED` cannot be set to anything except `false`; unknown truthy values also fail configuration. No feature flag overrides the database.
- Only an authenticated **adult pilot reviewer** can read `GET /api/pilot/guardian-readiness` while draft-2 is enabled. It returns metadata and the eight outstanding controls, never a child's information. There is **no POST endpoint** for guardianship or child identity. The adult invitation flow still requires `adultAttested: true` and a private operator's invitation; it cannot verify the declaration. Operators must not invite minors.
- Learner and family demo routes do **not** create pilot users or a real guardian-child relationship. Never transfer local preview data into the PostgreSQL pilot as official evidence.

## Approval gates before writing child data or allowing child access

Every gate needs a named accountable owner, a documented outcome, and a review date. A single approved control is insufficient; a later versioned policy/migration must implement all of the following and pass an independent release review. Ghana-specific legal and age-of-consent advice must be obtained from qualified local counsel and the designated privacy/safeguarding leads; international expansion requires per-jurisdiction review rather than reusing a Ghana consent text everywhere.

1. **Legal basis and Ghana review:** define data-controller/processor roles, lawful basis, age/consent requirements, notice language, cross-border transfers, records of processing and a child-impact/privacy assessment. Decide what a school may do on a guardian's behalf; do not assume a teacher or school automatically has authority to consent.
2. **Age assurance:** decide an accessible, proportionate method for determining an adult account versus a minor, handling uncertain ages and avoiding unnecessary birth dates. The existing checkbox cannot serve as verification.
3. **Guardian authority verification:** prove an adult is authorised for the particular child before linking identities. Review provider, evidence strength, expiry, re-verification, disputes, multiple guardians and court/child-protection exceptions. Never accept a browser-supplied guardian flag as proof.
4. **Purpose-scoped consent and withdrawal:** explain separate purposes (core learning, portfolios, optional uploads, communications and research/analytics), collect affirmative choices tied to a published notice/policy version and proof, and support withdrawal without coercion. Optional scopes must not become prerequisites for the 44-course sequence. A withdrawn/expired authority must revoke access and sessions before further child writes, with a reviewed retention/deletion policy.
5. **Child-identity minimisation:** after approval, use opaque subject IDs and age bands where possible; do not request public profiles, contact details, full DOB or IDs in lesson submissions. Evidence should remain with an approved specialist provider or an encrypted, access-controlled store with a strict TTL—not raw files in learning tables, logs or browser storage.
6. **Safeguarding response:** assign trained responders and escalation/incident procedures; prevent unrestricted child–adult messaging, open publication of projects and direct contact by partners. Review age-appropriate reporting/help UI, guardian visibility and mandatory-reporting obligations with counsel.
7. **Retention, access and erasure:** define data inventories, guardian/child access and correction workflows, preservation exceptions, deletion windows, backup expiry, audit evidence separation and school-tenant boundaries. Enforce with server-side authorisation and test cross-school isolation.
8. **Independent security review:** MFA for sensitive adult roles, recovery, privacy/accessibility/security testing, abuse controls, audit retention, encrypted secrets, backups/restores, incident simulation and a two-person release sign-off. Confirm WCAG 2.2 AA with manual assistive-technology testing; automated axe tests alone are not certification.

## Proposed later state machine (not implemented)

`UNVERIFIED_ADULT → VERIFIED_ADULT → AUTHORITY_PENDING → AUTHORITY_VERIFIED → CONSENT_PENDING → CONSENT_ACTIVE → CHILD_ACCESS_ELIGIBLE`, with `REJECTED`, `EXPIRED`, `DISPUTED` and `WITHDRAWN` transitions. A child account, session, course enrolment or protected submission may exist **only** when all server-owned gates are current for that child, guardian, jurisdiction, purpose and policy version; a school role cannot impersonate a guardian. Verification and consent are separate: email control, adult age, legal authority, child assent when appropriate, and scoped consent must not be conflated.

A later evidence design should use append-only, time-stamped consent/withdrawal events with policy hash, jurisdiction, scope, verified authority reference, actor and expiry. Store the minimum necessary proof reference, not uploaded identity documents. Revoke child sessions and suspend processing immediately on withdrawal, dispute, expiry, failed checks or policy change. Restrict reviewer lookup by assignment and tenant; audit every access without writing secrets or child content into logs. Require independent human handling for conflicting guardians and safeguarding cases; do not auto-approve based on a single checkbox or school invitation.

## Future acceptance tests and release decision

- Deny direct URLs/API mutations when any gate is missing, expired, disputed, withdrawn, wrong tenant or tied to an old policy; repeated/replayed evidence cannot unlock another child.
- Verify a valid guardian's scoped consent activates only allowed functions; removing an optional scope cannot block required learning. Withdrawal revokes sessions, pauses progression and follows approved deletion/retention rules.
- Prove uploaded evidence does not leak to logs, the browser bundle, analytics, partner dashboards or other tenants; exercise access/correction/erasure and recovery with synthetic child records in an isolated test environment only.
- Security, privacy, legal, safeguarding and accessibility owners sign the precise version of the policy, migration, UI and operational runbook. Until then, the release gate remains **blocked**; this document and its eight rows are not an approved consent solution.
